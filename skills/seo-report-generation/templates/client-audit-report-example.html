Executive summary

Sommerfield has local authority. The website is not converting that authority into a clean location-and-language growth system.

Cheras is already competitive in Google’s organic and map results, while Bangsar has meaningful local-pack visibility. The next phase should not begin with generic blog production. It should establish dedicated facility pages, recover the existing Chinese site’s indexation, remove legacy sibling-brand content, and make enquiries measurable.

Implementation roadmap

A 90-day plan focused on commercial ownership, not content volume.

Weeks 1–2

Clean the entity and tracking

Remove incorrect sibling-brand/location references, resolve legal-page ownership, fix duplicate form firing, validate WhatsApp/phone tracking, repair the broken link, and decide the canonical URL for duplicate page groups.

Weeks 2–5

Create facility owner pages

Launch unique Cheras and Bangsar pages with NAP, rooms, services, nearby hospitals, photographs, reviews, FAQs, schema and visit/availability CTAs. Point each GBP to the appropriate facility page.

Weeks 3–7

Recover Chinese discovery

Correct translation and brand defects, add Chinese URLs to the sitemap, validate hreflang, submit in GSC, request indexation and monitor Chinese-prefix impressions separately.

Weeks 6–12

Expand high-intent services

Build or strengthen pages for dementia/memory care, bedridden nursing, respite/short stay, post-hospital rehabilitation and day care—only where the service is genuinely available and operationally supported.

Responsibilities

Sommerfield team

Approve facility facts, legal entity wording, services, clinical claims, room information, pricing approach, photography and Chinese terminology.

Website implementation

Build pages, redirects, metadata, sitemap, hreflang, schema and analytics changes; verify rendered HTML on mobile and desktop.

SEO & admissions measurement

Track rankings, GSC coverage, enquiries, facility visits and admissions; maintain reason-lost data so growth decisions move beyond button clicks.

90-day acceptance checklist

  • Cheras and Bangsar have separate indexable owner pages
  • Each GBP points to its matching facility page
  • Priority Chinese URLs are known and indexed in GSC
  • No incorrect Woodrose/Shah Alam references remain on Sommerfield commercial pages
  • Form submission fires once per successful submission
  • WhatsApp, phone and form actions are tracked by facility and language
  • Organic reporting distinguishes contact actions, genuine enquiries, visits and admissions
Methodology and limitations

Evidence used

This audit combines live public crawling, WordPress REST and sitemap checks, Google Search Console performance and URL Inspection, GA4 organic traffic and key-event data, and a fixed 20-keyword mobile SERP panel localized to Kuala Lumpur, Cheras and Bangsar. Search-volume estimates are Malaysia-level directional values. Local and organic rankings can vary by device, date and exact user location.

Important: A WhatsApp click, form event or phone click is not automatically a genuine enquiry, facility visit or admission. No website, analytics, Search Console, Google Business Profile or publishing changes were made as part of this audit.
